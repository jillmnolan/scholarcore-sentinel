# Threat Model

Sentinel may operate with intermittent connectivity, hostile networks, lost or stolen devices, limited power, constrained memory, and outdated infrastructure.

Assets include local knowledge, user records, cryptographic material, model files, location data, and device configuration. Initial research considers theft, malicious networks, compromised dependencies, corruption, unauthorized local access, and cloud-service failure. Nation-state hardware attacks and certified high-assurance threat models are out of scope for the initial prototype.
