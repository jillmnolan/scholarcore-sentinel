# Hardware Selection

The initial study targets inexpensive prepaid Android phones because they combine ARM compute, battery, display, radios, sensors, and storage at unusually low cost.

Initial candidates are the Motorola Moto G (2025) 5G 64 GB and TCL K33 5G 64 GB. Priority is given to physical RAM, processor capability, battery, microSD, radio support, USB connectivity, update availability, and acquisition cost. Virtual RAM must be recorded separately from physical memory.
