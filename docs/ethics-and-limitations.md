# Ethics and Limitations

ScholarCore Sentinel is intended for lawful research into resilient, privacy-preserving computing. It prioritizes user-controlled data, offline capability, affordability, accessibility, honest documentation of limitations, and respect for third-party licenses.

Low-cost mobile hardware imposes severe memory, compute, thermal, storage, and battery constraints. Local AI output may be incorrect. Sentinel is not a substitute for professional medical, emergency, legal, or public-safety guidance.
